Pharmacy POS v6.6 FINAL

Billing flow changes:
- POS stays open after a bill is completed.
- After sale/receipt, cart is cleared and cursor returns to medicine/barcode search.
- Keyboard-first flow:
  1. Scan/type barcode or medicine and press Enter.
  2. Press Enter on empty search to move to Payment Method.
  3. Press Enter on Payment Method to move to Amount Received.
  4. Enter received amount and press Enter to Complete Sale.
  5. Receipt opens/prints; POS remains open ready for the next bill.
- Existing 3-inch/80mm receipt, barcode, reports, admin, expiry, and v6.5 fixes retained.
